Implemented flyback diodes to clamp inductive voltage spikes, sized current-limiting resistors for worst-case loads, and analyzed DC power supply ripple. Hands-on experience using standard test bench equipment, including oscilloscopes, multimeters, and DC power supplies for circuit troubleshooting and hardware verification.
