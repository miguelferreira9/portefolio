EV Powertrain Cooling Development

CAD design and physical assembly of the powertrain cooling system for the TEK 26 Formula Student prototype, including custom hose fittings, adapters, and fan mounting brackets.

Design & Validation Strategy

SolidWorks CAD

Modeled custom hose fittings, adapters, and fan brackets in SolidWorks. Designed connector geometries with smooth transitions—avoiding 90-degree sharp bends—to ensure negligible pressure drop at the pump's 10 L/min flow rate

FDM Rapid Prototyping

Utilized FDM 3D printing for rapid physical validation and fitment checks before committing to complex manufacturing. Printed test models in PETG for quick geometric iteration and functional cooling connectors in PA12, significantly reducing prototyping time and costly machining errors.
